How fees are worked out

Start = 1 full fee. Coming on at any point = ½ fee. Unused substitute = no fee.

*Fee units = starts + half of used-substitute appearances. Multiply by the player's agreed fee only after checking match coverage and any rate changes. Deductions and payments already made must then be reconciled separately.

A dash means the source field was blank, not a verified zero. Units are shown only where starts plus used substitutes match the published appearance total, treating blanks as zero for this provisional calculation.
